# Season projection benchmark

Recorded 2026-10-23 on an Apple M4 Mac16,1 (24 GB), macOS 27.0, Node 24.19.0, Rust 1.95.0,
wasm-bindgen 0.2.129, and Binaryen 133. The browser artifact was generated by
`bash scripts/build-bindings.sh --only wasm` and measured with `node bench/season-projection.mjs`.

Each strategy ran in a fresh process. “Cold” is its first projection journey after WASM initialization; initialization
itself is excluded. “Warm” is the median of nine journeys after three warmups. The fixtures and fixed `now` were
identical. Bytes are the total UTF-8 request plus response JSON for one journey.

| Episodes | Strategy | Calls | Serialized bytes | Cold ms | Warm median ms |
| ---: | --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| 24 | repeated `episode_state_v4` | 24 | 94,255 | 20.573 | 2.748 |
| 24 | batched `season_state_v4` | 1 | 7,400 | 55.357 | 0.879 |
| 100 | repeated `episode_state_v4` | 100 | 1,494,737 | 427.963 | 209.861 |
| 100 | batched `season_state_v4` | 1 | 29,926 | 53.378 | 1.749 |

The 24-episode cold batch was slower in this sample, so cold results should not be inferred from the warmed speedup.
These are operation measurements for one synthetic season, not real-TV timings or full-library journey results.

Allocation evidence comes from
`cargo test --locked -p den-sync --test season_projection_memory -- --nocapture`, using a counting native allocator
around equivalent 100-episode output:

| Strategy | Bytes allocated | Peak live heap bytes |
| --- | ---: | ---: |
| repeated | 67,967,594 | 462,153 |
| batched | 1,183,967 | 440,761 |

The allocation test asserts every batched state equals the corresponding single-episode state. It measures the native
JSON boundary, not WASM linear-memory growth or a full-library journey.
